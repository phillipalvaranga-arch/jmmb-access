Jamaica stays on Eastern Standard Time all year, so in winter it is 1 hour ahead of Central Time, and in summer it matches Central Daylight Time.

Safety tips

  • Check the address bar. Before you type anything, make sure the page is on a jmmb.com address, such as moneyline.jmmb.com or jm.jmmb.com. If the address looks different, close the tab and start again from this shortcut or from JMMB's official site.
  • JMMB will not ask for your password by email, text, or phone. JMMB's own security guidance says it will not ask you to disclose your PIN or password, and will not ask for account or personal details by email or SMS. If someone claiming to be JMMB asks for them, hang up or delete the message and call Client Care using the numbers on this page.
  • Turn on extra security (2FA). On JMMB's official Moneyline app, use the one-time passcode JMMB sends by text, and turn on fingerprint or face unlock if your phone offers it. Do not share that passcode with anyone.
  • This shortcut never collects your sign-in details. There are no username, password, PIN, account number, or one-time-passcode boxes here. If you ever see a page like that inside this app, it is not from this shortcut — close it.
